What is Nepal Lipi?

Nepal Lipi (𑐣𑐾𑐥𑐵𑐮 𑐮𑐶𑐥𑐶), also known as Prachalit Nepal Lipi, is the traditional script of Nepal Bhasa. Used extensively across Kathmandu Valley for centuries in stone inscriptions, manuscripts, and literature, it is encoded in Unicode standard (U+11480–U+114DF).

Real-Time Conversion

This converter maps Devanagari phonemes directly to Unicode Nepal Lipi code points. Special conjuncts (such as ङ्ह, ञ्ह, र्ह) are parsed accurately to maintain authentic manuscript orthography in real-time.
